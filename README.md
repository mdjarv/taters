# Potato Crop Mod for Vintage Story

The **Potato Crop Mod** introduces potatoes as a new crop to Vintage Story, expanding the agricultural gameplay in a fun and immersive way. Designed with realism in mind, this mod provides players with an exciting opportunity to grow, harvest, and use potatoes in their survival adventures.

---

## Features

- **New Crop**: Adds potatoes to the game as a plantable and harvestable crop.
- **Dynamic Growth Stages**: Potatoes progress through multiple growth stages, with visible changes during each phase.
- **Immersive Farming**: Potatoes require proper care and conditions to thrive, providing a rewarding farming experience.
- **Harvestable Rewards**: Fully grown potato crops yield both potatoes for consumption or crafting and seeds for replanting.

---

## Farming Potatoes

1. **Start Planting**:
   - Potato seeds can be found in loot containers, purchased from traders, or harvested from mature potato crops.
2. **Prepare the Soil**:
   - Tilled soil is required to plant your potato seeds.
3. **Care for Your Crops**:
   - Potatoes grow over time when conditions are met.
4. **Reap the Rewards**:
   - Harvest potatoes and seeds when the crops mature.

---

## Why Add Potatoes?

Potatoes are a staple crop with a rich history, valued for their versatility and resilience. By introducing potatoes, this mod adds depth to farming mechanics while providing a reliable food source. Players will need to balance their soil use, monitor growth conditions, and tend to their crops for optimal results.

---

## How to Install

1. Download the mod files from the release page.
2. Place the `.zip` file into the `Mods` folder located in the Vintage Story installation directory.
3. Enable the mod via the in-game mod manager.
4. Restart the game after enabling the mod.
